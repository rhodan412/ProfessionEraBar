# Changelog

## v12.1.0.0 — Initial release (2026-10-06)

- Added a profession expansion selector beside the Retail Professions window for one-click era switching.
- Highlighted the selected era and dimmed unlearned eras for the current profession.
- Added skill level and available specialization points to era tooltips.
- Supported switching from Recipes and Specializations, returning to Specializations when the chosen era has a specialization tree.
- Positioned the bar on the side of the Professions window with available screen space and hid it on unsupported tabs and during NPC crafting.
- Added a movable hourglass launcher: left click opens the first primary profession, right click opens the second, Ctrl + left click toggles locking, and Shift + drag resizes it when unlocked.
- Saved the launcher's position, size, and lock state between sessions.

For World of Warcraft Retail (Interface 120100).
