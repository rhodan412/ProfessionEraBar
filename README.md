# Profession Era Bar

*Switch profession expansions with one click in World of Warcraft Retail.*

Profession Era Bar places a compact, twelve-era selector beside the Professions window. Choose a learned era to open that expansion of your current profession. The selected era is highlighted; unlearned eras stay visible in grayscale with an "Unlearned" tooltip. The bar moves to the side of the window with available screen space.

## What it does

- **Keeps all twelve eras in view.** Learned expansion tiers are in color and selectable. Unlearned tiers are grayscale, show their expansion name and unlearned status on hover, and cannot be selected.
- **Works from Recipes and Specializations.** When you choose an era with a specialization tree from Specializations, the window returns there after the switch. Eras without a tree open in Recipes.
- **Shows useful details on hover.** Learned eras show skill level and, when available, unspent specialization points.
- **Stays out of the way.** The bar chooses the side of the Professions window with room and hides on unsupported tabs or during NPC crafting.

## Get started

1. [Install Profession Era Bar from CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/profession-era-bar) and enable it for your Retail character. For manual installation, place the `ProfessionEraBar` folder in `World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns/`.
2. Open a primary profession with the hourglass launcher or the game's normal profession controls.
3. On **Recipes** or **Specializations**, click a colored era button beside the Professions window to switch that profession's expansion tier.

If an era is grayscale, that character has not learned that expansion tier for the current profession. Hover it to see its name and status.

## Launcher controls

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Left click | Open the first primary profession's Recipes. |
| Right click | Open the second primary profession's Recipes. |
| Ctrl + left click | Lock or unlock the launcher. |
| Drag while unlocked | Move the launcher. |
| Shift + drag while unlocked | Resize the launcher. |

The launcher remembers its position, size, and lock state between sessions. See the [Wiki](https://github.com/rhodan412/ProfessionEraBar/wiki) for the full controls and troubleshooting guide.
